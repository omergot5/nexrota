Covered structurally by D-03's default-allow rule: `isQualified`/`checkQualification` treat absent, `null`, or empty `qualifiedCategories` as qualified for everything (`QUAL-02` — 6+ assertions across `verify-planning.mjs`/`verify-scheduler.mjs`, including the mapper-crossing case where a row that predates the migration entirely, missing the column, also resolves to unrestricted). **Not independently re-driven live this session** with a brand-new team — the existing demo team already carries assignments from prior sessions.
