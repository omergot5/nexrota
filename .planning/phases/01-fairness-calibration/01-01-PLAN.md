<objective>
Move the engine's fairness measurement into weighted-load units and make the calibration self-proving.

Two places in `src/lib/autoAssign.js` still measure shift count: `balanceWorkload`'s guard ranking and stopping check (lines 522–532), and `buildResult`'s `fairnessScore` (lines 633–642). This plan converts both, re-derives the two constants that were tuned for count units, and adds structural regression checks that fail `npm test` if the reported score ever stops matching the reported load.

Purpose: the number on screen must be the number the engine divided by. A transparency feature that reports a wrong figure is worse than no transparency, because it buys trust it cannot honour (D-01).

Output: a load-calibrated engine plus new `FAIR-0N ·` assertions in `scripts/verify-scheduler.mjs`.

**Decision IDs** (assigned from `01-CONTEXT.md` § "הכרעות נעולות שרלוונטיות לפאזה", which is a table without IDs):
- **D-01** — load is the only fairness metric in the product
- **D-02** — the constants are re-derived, not token-swapped
- **D-03** — the participant sees the same number the engine divided by (covered in plan 01-02)
- **D-04** — absolute determinism: no RNG, no clock dependence, no DB row-order dependence
- **D-05** — the design system is mandatory on every screen this phase touches (covered in plan 01-02)
</objective>
