<objective>
Wire the unified engine into every screen that asks it a question, and make the counted/frozen distinction visible to the manager.

Plan 02-01 taught the engine to measure tasks and plan 02-02 taught the product to store their hours. Neither is worth anything until the six call sites that ask "may this person take this?" and "how much has this person carried?" actually pass the tasks along. `02-RESEARCH.md` § "Pitfall 1" and § "Pitfall 2" enumerate them with file and line; missing even one reopens the exact defect this milestone exists to close — a number on screen that the engine did not divide by.

Purpose: UNIF-02's second half and the whole of UNIF-05. A transparency feature that reports a figure the engine never used is worse than no transparency, because it buys trust it cannot honour.

Output: a `tasks` prop threaded from the two app shells down to the engine and the reporting functions, every merge going through the one shared helper, a frozen badge on the only surface that renders an individual task, and an honest counted-versus-frozen line on the dashboard.

**Decision IDs.** Numbering and full text are in `02-01-PLAN.md` § objective.

- **D-03** — the badge appears only on frozen tasks. An active task is the common case and is meant to look like an ordinary duty; a badge on everything is noise that stops being read
- **D-04** — the badge appears everywhere a frozen task is displayed, bounded by D-08
- **D-07** — a task's load weight is flat, so a screen never needs to explain a task-specific multiplier
- **D-08** *(resolved here, `02-RESEARCH.md` Open Question 2 and Pitfall 7)* — **there is no task card on any calendar today.** `WeekCalendar.jsx` and `CalendarView.jsx` were grepped case-insensitively for task references during research and again during planning: zero matches in either file. Neither renders a task at all, so there is no calendar card to carry a badge. D-04 is therefore satisfied in full by badging every surface that renders an individual task — which today is exactly one, the task row inside the tasks screen — plus an aggregate counted-versus-frozen line on the dashboard, whose task display is a single number rather than a list of items. This is a scope boundary, not a gap: building a task card on the calendar is new UI that nobody asked for in this phase, and the unified board that will render shifts and tasks together is already scoped as `BOARD-01` in Phase 5. If the developer wants a calendar card sooner, that is a roadmap change, not a silent addition here
</objective>
