<planner_decisions>
- **P-01 — the "יומן" tab keeps a week mode, repointed at `UnifiedBoard`.** Three options were live; this plan adopts the repoint. Deleting the toggle outright would silently remove an entry point a manager may already use, and BOARD-04 punishes surprise. Leaving `WeekCalendar` mounted there would leave the app with two week views that disagree about whether tasks exist, which is the precise failure BOARD-01 names. Repointing keeps the affordance and collapses the truth to one source. `CalendarView` (month) is untouched — a month grid answers a different question and is not in this phase's scope.
- **Reversibility gate.** CONTEXT.md rates D-01 `costly`: reversing it means restoring deleted code, not flipping a setting. Task 1 is therefore a `checkpoint:decision` placed *before* the task that deletes the file, so the deletion is a human's call at the moment it happens rather than an inference from a document written days earlier.
- **BOARD-04's acceptance is a human observation, and it stays one.** `05-VALIDATION.md` records it as manual-only by the phase's own success criteria. `workflow.human_verify_mode` is `end-of-phase`, so it is carried as a `<human-check>` on the final task and harvested into the phase UAT rather than halting mid-plan.
</planner_decisions>
